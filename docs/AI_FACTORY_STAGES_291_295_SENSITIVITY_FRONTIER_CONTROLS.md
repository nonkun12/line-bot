# AI Factory Stages 291–295: Sensitivity, Frontier and Control Evidence

- **291 — Threshold sensitivity:** re-evaluates per-domain regression outcomes across a declared regression-budget grid; fragile conclusions are exposed.
- **292 — Evaluator drift:** checks chronological per-evaluator calibration-error movement and flags drift beyond a declared limit.
- **293 — Capability frontier:** records scores against caller-declared complexity levels and flags excessive adjacent-level drops per domain.
- **294 — Matched control comparison:** subtracts matched control change from candidate change and requires a minimum net gain over at least three pairs.
- **295 — Research-readiness integration:** requires all four sealed gates and returns eligibility for separate independent review only.

## Limitations

Inputs, task identities, complexity labels, timestamps, evaluator IDs, scores and digests are caller-supplied. Hashes do not authenticate custody, actor identity, evaluator independence, task blinding, randomization or causal identification. Stage 294 is a descriptive matched-control contrast, not proof of causal effect. Stage 293 does not establish a universal capability scale. None of these stages proves general intelligence, recursive self-improvement or ASI.

## Safety

All outputs are proposal/evidence-only. Task execution, experiments, model changes/training, HOLD release, merge and deployment remain unauthorized. All authorization flags remain false.
