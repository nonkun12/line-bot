# AI Factory Stages 296–300: Evidence Quality and Capability Gates

- **296 — Evidence completeness:** requires unique digest-bound records and declared required fields.
- **297 — Uncertainty quality:** validates bounded per-domain intervals and flags excessive widths.
- **298 — Task-family coverage:** requires exact declared family coverage and minimum counts per family.
- **299 — Cross-run consistency:** checks repeated domain score summaries for domain parity and score spread.
- **300 — Integrated capability evidence:** requires all four sealed checks and yields eligibility for separate independent review only.

## Limitations

All records, fields, task families, intervals, scores, timestamps and digests are caller-supplied. Hashes do not authenticate provenance, custody, timestamps, actors, evaluator independence, environment parity, task representativeness or statistical coverage. Interval construction and coverage guarantees are not independently verified. Cross-run consistency does not prove independent replications or causal improvement. These stages do not prove general capability gain, recursive self-improvement or ASI.

## Safety

Proposal/evidence-only. Task execution, experiments, model changes/training, HOLD release, merge and deployment remain unauthorized. All authorization flags remain false.
