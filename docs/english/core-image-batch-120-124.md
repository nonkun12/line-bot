# English core-image batch 120–124

**Status:** proposal on a feature branch; not registered on `main` until merged. This numbering follows the 119-word main dictionary snapshot at the start of the batch.

## 120 — break / ブレイク

**Core image:** a continuous thing is split or stopped at a point → break, interrupt, violate.

- `break a promise`: 約束を破る
- `break the silence`: 沈黙を破る
- `break down`: 分解する／故障する（文脈で変わる）
- `break through`: 障害を突破する

Example: *Let's break the problem down into smaller steps.*

## 121 — build / ビルド

**Core image:** parts or effort are accumulated into a structure or foundation → construct, develop, strengthen.

- `build a house/team`: 建物を建てる／チームを作る
- `build trust`: 信頼を築く
- `build up confidence`: 自信を高める

Example: *We are building trust one step at a time.*

## 122 — change / チェンジ

**Core image:** move from one state to another → change, become different.

- `change one's mind`: 考えを変える
- `change A into B`: AをBに変える
- `change for the better/worse`: 良い／悪い方向へ変わる

Example: *She changed her mind after the meeting.*

## 123 — close / クローズ（verb）

**Core image:** close an opening or bring something to an end → close, finish, narrow.

- `close the door/window`: 閉める
- `close a meeting`: 会議を締めくくる
- `close a business`: 営業を終える
- `close the gap`: 差を縮める

Example: *Please close the window before you leave.*

**Sound distinction:** verb `close` /z/ (クローズ); adjective `close` “near” /s/ (クロース). This batch registers the verb.

## 124 — find / ファインド

**Core image:** reach the sought object, location, answer, or realization → find, discover, realize, judge.

- `find a way`: 方法を見つける
- `find out`: 調べて知る
- `find it difficult`: 難しいと感じる

Example: *I found it difficult to explain.*

## Usage contrast

- **break** focuses on breaking/interrupting/violating; phrase context strongly changes its meaning.
- **build** focuses on accumulating to create or strengthen; **make** is broader for creating something.
- **change** moves a state to another; it does not necessarily mean replacing an object.
- **close** is the opposite of open for a door, but can also end an event/business or reduce a gap. **shut** often emphasizes the closing action.
- **find** is reaching a discovery/result; **look for** is the search process, while **find out** is learning a fact.
